The relations $z=f(x,y)$ and $g(x,y,z)=c$ define a surface in 3D 
Can sketch contours with x, y or z fixed to determine nature of stationary points 
To do partial derivatives, treat every variable except for respected one as a constant 
e.g. for $z=xy^2-4x^2y+y+20:\frac{\delta z}{\delta x}=y^2-8xy$ 

Stationary point when $\frac{\delta z}{\delta x}=0$ and $\frac{\delta z}{\delta y}=0$ 
Stationary points are either maxima, minima or saddle points 

The grad function $\nabla g$ gives the normal vector to the plane at any given point 
Can use to find tangential plane or stationary points when $x$ and $y$ in the normal are 0
