Centre of mass in $x,y,z$ axes are given as $\bar{x},\bar{y},\bar{z}$ 
To find centre of mass, use moments about a point : 
Centre of mass $\bar{x}=\frac{1}{M}(x_1m_1+x_2m_2+x_3m_3+...)$ where $M=\sum{m_i}$ or $\bar{x}\sum{m_i}=\sum{x_im_i}$
