#Mathematics #Further #Mechanics 

Work = Force $\times$ distance ; $W = Fd$ 
Mechanical energy is the sum of kinetic and potential energies 
Kinetic energy $KE=\frac{1}{2}mv^2$ 
Gravitational potential energy $GPE=mgh$ 

A conservative force is independent of the path taken e.g. gravitational force 
A dissipative force is non-conservative that decreases mechanical energy e.g. friction 
A driving force accelerates an object in a specific direction 
A resistive force opposes the direction of motion of an object 
The power of a force is the rate at which work is done by it 
The power developed by a vehicle is the available power / the power of the driving force 

Work-energy principle : Work done by all external forces is equal to increase of KE of a body 

Average power = work done $\div$ elapsed time $P=\frac{W}{t}$ 
Power = force $\times$ velocity in direction of force $P=Fv$
