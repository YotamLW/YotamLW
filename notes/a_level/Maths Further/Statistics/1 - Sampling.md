#Mathematics #Further #Statistics 

Sample size can affect the accuracy and precision of experimental design 
Larger sample sizes also tend to increase statistical significance 
Advantages of larger samples : 
- More representative of population 
- Random variation in sample tends to decrease 
- Calculated sample statistics are closer to population statistics 
- Influence of outliers is reduced 

A sample can be considered as $n$ observations from a random variable 
Sampling is necessary when :
- The population is too large 
- A census is too time consuming and expensive 
- Sampling process is destructive 

Desirable features of a sample : 
- Unbiased 
- Representative of population 
- Relevant 
- Unchanged by act of sampling 

A random sample enables proper inference about the population to be undertaken because the probability basis on which the sample has been selected is known
