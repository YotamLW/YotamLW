#Physics #Electric 

Electric Potential ($V$) is the work done to bring a unit positive charge from infinity to a point 
$V = \frac{Q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0r}$ as $r \rightarrow \infty, V \rightarrow 0$ 
Where $Q$ is the charge producing the electric field 
Moving towards a charge decreases potential, Moving away increases potential 
Electric potential difference is work done per unit charge to move a charged particle 

For an isolated sphere, capacitance $C = 4\pi\varepsilon_0R$ where $R$ is the radius 
$V = \frac{Q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0r}$ so $Q=4\pi\varepsilon_0RV$ and $C=\frac{Q}{V}$ so $C=\frac{4\pi\varepsilon_0RV}{V}=4\pi\varepsilon_0R$ 

Electric potential energy is $V q = \frac{Qq}{4\pi\varepsilon_0r}$
