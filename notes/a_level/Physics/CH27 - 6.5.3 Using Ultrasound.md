Ultrasound is longitudinal sound waves with frequency greater than 20 kHz 

The piezoelectric effect is when crystals such as quartz produce an e.m.f. when a force is applied 
Polarity of induced e.m.f. depends on directions of the force (e.g. compressed and stretched) 
Applying an e.m.f. to the crystal will also cause it to deform

An ultrasound transducer is a device that emits and receives ultrasound 
To produce ultrasound : 
- The transducer applies a high frequency alternating p.d. on the crystal 
- The frequency matches the crystal's natural frequency so that it resonates 
- This emits pulses of ultrasound 
To recieve ultrasound : 
- Between pulses, incoming ultrasound makes the crystal vibrate which produces an e.m.f. 
- Circuits detect this e.m.f. which can then be used to generate images

An A-scan uses a 1D scan to determine depth 
An A-scan can be used to determine depth of components of the eye 
At a boundary between media, some of the ultrasound will be reflected and some will continue 
The time interval on a voltage-time graph is the time for the ultrasound to go out and return 
![[]] 

A B-scan is when the transducer is moved over the skin to generate a 2D image 
For each position, a row of dots is made which are processed to produce an image 
The image is in the same plane as the ultrasound lines made by the transducer

Acoustic impedance $Z=\rho c$ where $\rho$ is density of the medium and $c$ is the speed of the ultrasound 
Ultrasound reflection at boundaries affects intensity 
$\frac{I_r}{I_0}=\frac{(Z_2-Z_1)^2}{(Z_2+Z_1)^2}$ where $I_0$ is initial intensity, $I_r$ is reflected intensity and $Z_1$ is the initial medium 
![[]] 

Acoustic / impedance matching is when two substances have similar acoustic impedances 
This means reflected intensity is negligible 
A coupling gel is used in ultrasound scanning that matches with skin's acoustic impedance 

Doppler effect in ultrasound can be used to monitor the speed of blood in a patient 
The equation $\frac{\Delta f}{f}=\frac{2v\cos\theta}{c}$ is used to determine speed of blood 
- $f$ is the normal ultrasound frequency 
- $\Delta f$ is the change in frequency from the normal frequency 
- $c$ is the speed of the ultrasound 
- $v$ is the speed of the blood 
- $\theta$ is the angle between the blood vessel and the direction of the transducer
