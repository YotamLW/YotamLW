A potential divider circuit is used to vary p.d. of components 
Use $\frac{V_1}{V_2} = \frac{R_1}{R_2}$ to find p.d. on different components 
Another equation is $V_{out} = V_{in} \left( \frac{R_2}{R_1 + R_2} \right)$ where $V_{out}$ is over $R_2$ 
![[4.3.3a]] 

Components may include resistors, variable resistors, LDRs or thermistors 
Which component $V_{out}$ is applied on is based on the context of the question 

A potentiometer can be used as a potential divider with only 1 component
