#Physics #Nuclear 

**Alpha Scattering Experiment** : 
- Alpha particles shot at thin gold foil and detected after 
- Most went straight through undeflected 
- Very few deflected backwards 
This concluded that : 
- Most of the atom was empty space as most particles went through 
- Small concentrated mass (nucleus) 
- Nucleus has positive charge as alpha particles deflected 

Nucleon is either a proton or neutron 
An Isotope is an element with different numbers of neutrons 
A Nucleus is represented as $_Z ^A X$, X = symbol, A = nucleon number, Z = atomic number 
Radius of a nucleus $R = r_0 A^\frac{1}{3}$ where $r_0 \approx 1.2 fm$ (radius of a proton) 
Density of nuclei $\approx 10^{17} kg m^{-3}$ 
Density of atoms $\approx 10^{3} kg m^{-3}$ 

The Strong Nuclear force keeps nucleons together 
Very short range, attractive to about ~3$fm$ and repulsive below ~0.5$fm$
