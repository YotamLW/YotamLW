#Physics #Radial 

A constant net force acting perpendicular to the velocity of an object will cause it to travel in a circular path (centripetal force) 

Speed in circular motion is constant so $v = \omega r$ 
Centripetal acceleration $a = \omega^2 r$ or $a = \frac{v^2}{r}$ 
Centripetal force $F = m \omega^2 r$ or $F = \frac{m v^2}{r}$ 

Can use a whirling bung to investigate circular motion
