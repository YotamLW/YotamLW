#Physics #Electric 

Mean drift velocity is the average velocity of charge carriers in a system that produces a current 

$I = Anev$ : 
- $I$ is the current of the system 
- $A$ is the cross-sectional area of the charge carriers perpendicular to direction of current 
- $n$ is the number density of the charge carriers (constant property) 
- $e$ is the elementary charge 
- $v$ is the mean drift velocity 

Number density is the number of free electrons / charge carriers per metre cubed 
How conductive a material is depends on number density : 
- Insulators - low number density ~ $10^8$ 
- Semiconductors - medium number density ~ $10^{17}$ 
- Conductors - high number density ~ $10^{28}$
