#Physics #Electric

Resistivity is a property of a material with units $\ohm m$ 
Resistivity $\rho = \frac{RA}{L}$ where $R$ is resistance, $A$ is area and $L$ is length 

Finding resistivity investigation : 
- Connect variable resistor to a circuit 
- Measure resistance for varying lengths of resistor 
- Allow to cool between measurements 
- Plot a Resistance on Length graph 
- Gradient is $\frac{\rho}{A}$ so $\rho = \text{gradient} \times A$ 

As temperature increase, resistivity of a material increases 

Resistivity of materials at 20 $^\circ C$ : 
- Conductors ~ $10^{-8} \; \ohm m$ 
- Insulators ~ $10^{16} \; \ohm m$ 
- Semiconductors - somewhere in-between 

Thermistors have a negative temperature coefficient 
This means resistance is inversely proportional to temperature
