#Physics #Energy 

Work done = Force $\times$ distance : $W = Fd$ 
A Joule is the work done when a force of 1 N moves an object by 1 m 
$J = Nm = kgm^2s^{-2}$ 

Principle of Conservation of Energy : 
The total energy of a closed system remains constant (energy cannot be created or destroyed) 

The energy of systems with mass can be Kinetic (KE) or Gravitation Potential (GPE) 
Energy is transferred between forms of energy to maintain conservation of energy 
Transfer of energy is equal to work done 
Energy can also be lost to the surroundings through thermal energy or kinetic energy
