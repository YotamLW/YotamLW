#Physics #Electric 

Current is the rate of flow of charge : $I = \frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta t}$ 
The unit of current is Amperes (A) 
The unit of charge is Coulombs (C) 

Charge is a physical property and can be positive, negative or 0 
Charge carriers are particles with charge 
The elementary charge $e$ is equal to $1.6 \times 10^{-19} C$ 
This is the charge on a single electron & proton 
All charge is in multiples of the elementary charge so is quantised 

Current is caused by the movement of charged particles 
Current is caused by delocalised electrons moving in metals 
Current is caused by movement of ions in electrolytes 
Conventional current is in the opposite direction to the flow of electrons 

Kirchhoff's first law (conservation of charge) : 
The sum of charge into a point = the sum of charge out of the point ($\Sigma Q_{\text{in}} = \Sigma Q_{\text{out}}$)
