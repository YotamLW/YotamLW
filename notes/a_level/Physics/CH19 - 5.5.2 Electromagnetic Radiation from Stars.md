#Physics #Astophysics

Electrons are bound to discrete energy levels in atoms 
- Cannot have quantity of energy between 2 levels 
- Energy levels are negative (external energy required to remove electron) 
- 0 energy means free from atom 
- Ground state is lowest energy value (closest to nucleus) 

An atom is said to be excited when an electron moves up an energy level 
When an electron moves down an energy level a photon is emitted 
Use equation $\Delta E = hf$ or $\Delta E = \frac{hc}{\lambda}$ 

Emission line spectrum : 
- Line spectrum created when photons of certain energy levels are emitted 
- Line spectrum unique to each element so can be identified from a spectrum 
Continuous spectrum : 
- Atoms of a heated solid produce this (e.g. metal) 
Absorption line spectrum : 
- Line spectra created when photons are absorbed by a cool gas to excite electrons 
- Pattern is like an inverted emission line spectra 
- Used to detect star composition from photons generated in the core 

Diffraction Grating used to determine wavelength 
Determine wavelength using $d sin\theta = n \lambda$ where $d$ is grating spacing 

Wein's displacement law : $\lambda_{max} \propto \frac{1}{T}$ 
Stefan's law $L = 4 \pi r^2 \sigma T^4$ where $\sigma$ is Stefan's constant 
This is used to estimate a star's peak surface temperature 

Black body radiation is the emission of wavelengths of a black body at different temperatures
