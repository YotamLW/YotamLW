Resistance $R = \frac{V}{I}$ or $V=IR$ 
The unit of resistance is the ohm ($\Omega$) 
1 ohm is the resistance of a component when 1 V of p.d. is produced by 1 A of current 

Ohm's law : 
For a metallic conductor at constant temperature, current is directly proportional to p.d. 
Materials that obey this law are called ohmic 
As temperature rises, more frequent particle collisions absorb energy so higher resistance

To get resistance from a graph use $V = IR$ and not the gradient 

Diodes have polarity, meaning current can only flow one way (as shown in its I-V graph) 
A light emitting diode (LED) converts energy into light 

A light dependant resistor (LDR) changes resistance based on light intensity
