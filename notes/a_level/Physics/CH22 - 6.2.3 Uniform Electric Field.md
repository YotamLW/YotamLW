#Physics #Electric 

In a uniform field force is constant throughout 
This means constant acceleration on charged particles so SUVAT is appropriate 
$W = Fd$ so $(VQ) = (EQ)d$ so $E = \frac{V}{d}$ 

Capacitance depends on the Area of the capacitor plates, the distance between the plates and the conduciveness of the material between the plates 
$C \propto \frac{A}{d}$ and $C = \frac{\varepsilon_0 A}{d}$ where $\varepsilon_0$ is the permittivity of free space $\varepsilon_0 = 8.55 \times 10^{-12}$ 
When a material lies between the plates $C = \frac{\varepsilon_r \varepsilon_0 A}{d}$ where $\varepsilon_r$ is relative permittivity and $\varepsilon_r \geq 1$ 
For vacuum and air $\varepsilon_r = 1$ 
May also be written $C = \frac{\varepsilon A}{d}$ where $\varepsilon = \varepsilon_r \varepsilon_0$
