#Physics #Motion 

Newton's 3 laws of motion : 
1) An object will remain at rest or at constant motion unless acted upon by a resultant force 
2) The resultant force on an object is directly proportional to the rate of change of momentum 
3) When two objects interact, they exert a force of equal type and magnitude on one another but in opposite directions 

Momentum is a vector and is a product of mass and velocity : $p = mv$ 
Newton's second law : $F = \frac{\Delta p}{\Delta t}$ where $\Delta p$ = final momentum - initial momentum 
$F = ma$ is a special case of this where mass and acceleration are constant 

The impulse of a force is the product of the force and the time interval : $\text{Impulse} = F \Delta t$ 
So Impulse = change in momentum
