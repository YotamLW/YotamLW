#Physics #Medical 

An X-ray tube works by having an electron gun chain into a target metal which is the anode  
The anode emits X-ray radiation when the electrons decelerate 
![[]] 

Each electron that impacts the target metal produces one photon 
The maximum energy of the X-ray photon is the maximum kinetic energy of an electron ($eV$) 
Increasing current increases intensity 

X-ray attenuation (absorption) mechanisms are different ways the intensity of X-rays can diminish 
X-ray attenuation mechanisms in order from lowest to highest energy : 
- Simple scatter - X-ray is deflected by an electron 
- Photoelectric effect - X-ray is absorbed by a metal's surface electron to escape 
- Compton scattering - Some of X-ray absorbed to free electron but some is scattered 
- Pair production - X-ray turns into an electron and positron where excess energy = KE 
![[]] 

Intensity of X-rays falls exponentially with depth where $I=I_0\,e^{-\mu x}$ 
$\mu$ is the attenuation / absorption coefficient 

A contrast medium has a high attenuation coefficient so provides contrast in images 
Common contrast media are iodine and barium as they are quite harmless 
Iodine is used for identifying areas with insufficient blood flow 
Barium is used to image the digestive system  

Computerised Axial Tomography (CAT) scanning generates a 3D image using X-rays : 
- An X-ray tube rotates in a circle around the patient 
- The X-ray tube produces a thin fan-shaped X-ray beam 
- Array of detectors opposite the X-ray tube to capture the X-rays 
- A computer software creates a 2D cross-section using the intensity values at different positions 
- 2D slices are joined together to produce the final 3D image of the patient 
![[]] 

CAT scan vs X-ray scan : 
- A CAT scan creates a 3D image rather than 2D so it is easier to identify problems e.g. tumours 
- CAT scans can distinguish between different types of soft tissue 
- CAT scans are much more expensive and longer than a simple X-ray scan 
- CAT scans expose the patient to much more radiation than X-ray 
- You need to be still for longer for a CAT scan
