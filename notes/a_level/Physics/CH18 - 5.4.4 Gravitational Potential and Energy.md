GP ($V_g$) is the work done per unit mass to bring an object from infinity to that point 
Gravitational potential has a maximum value of 0 at an infinite distance otherwise is negative 
$V_g = - \frac{GM}{r}$ and as $r \rightarrow \infty, V_g \rightarrow 0$ 
Where $M$ is the mass producing the gravitational field 
Moving towards a mass decreases potential, Moving away increases potential 

Gravitational potential energy $E = mV_g = - \frac{GMm}{r}$

The escape velocity is the velocity a mass needs to have enough energy to escape the gravitational field / bring its gravitational potential to 0 
$\frac{1}{2} m v^2 = \frac{GMm}{r}$ so $v = \sqrt{\frac{2GM}{r}}$
