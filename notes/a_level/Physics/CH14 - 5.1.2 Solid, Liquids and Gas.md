#Physics #Thermal 

The kinetic model describes all substances as atoms / molecules that are arranged differently depending on the phase of the substance 
Solids : 
- Particles are arranged in a fixed 3D structure 
- Particles are close together 
- Strong forces of attraction 
- Particles can vibrate 
Liquids : 
- No fixed shape 
- Particles still close together 
- Still forces of attraction 
- Particles are free to move about 
Gases : 
- No fixed shape 
- Particles are far apart 
- No forces of attraction 
- Particles move at high speeds 

Brownian motion is the effect of seeing small objects seemingly move randomly but actually colliding with small unobservable particles like air 
Smoke particles in air under a microscope shows this effect

Internal energy is the sum of the kinetic and potential energies of particles in a system 
Absolute zero (0 K) is the minimum temperature and is where internal energy = 0 
As temperature rises, kinetic energy rises 
When changing phase, potential energy increases but temperature does not
