#Physics #Fundamentals 

S.I. base units : 
- Mass - kilogram (kg) 
- Length - metre (m) 
- Time - second (s) 
- Current - Amperes (A) 
- Temperature - Kelvin (K) 
- Quantity - Mol (mol) 

Homogeneity is when two units share the same S.I base units 
To check, use different equations from formula booklet
