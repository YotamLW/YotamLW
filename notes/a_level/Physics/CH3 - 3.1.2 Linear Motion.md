#Physics #Motion 

SUVAT equations are equations for motion of constant acceleration on a plane 
- s - displacement 
- u - initial velocity 
- v - final velocity 
- a - acceleration 
- t - time 

Equations without certain variable : 
- S : $v = u + at$ 
- U : $s = vt - \frac{1}{2} at^2$ 
- V : $s = ut + \frac{1}{2} at^2$ 
- A : $s = \frac{u + v}{2} t$ 
- T : $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$ 

Methods of investigating motion & collisions : 
- Trolleys 
- Air track gliders 
- Light gates & data loggers 

The acceleration $g$ of free fall is 9.81 $ms^{-2}$ 
Methods of determining $g$ : 
- Electromagnet & trapdoor 
- Light gates & data loggers 
- Camera 

Thinking distance is the distance travelled during the reaction time of a driver 
Braking distance is the distance travelled while the car is braking 
Stopping distance = thinking distance + braking distance
