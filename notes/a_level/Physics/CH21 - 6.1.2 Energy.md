#Physics #Electric 

$V \propto Q$ so area under graph of V on Q is the energy stored by a capacitor 
$W = \frac{1}{2} VQ$ or $W = \frac{1}{2} V^2 C$ or $W = \frac{1}{2} \frac{Q^2}{C}$ 
Capacitors can store energy 
![[6.1.2a]] 

Used for applications that require very high Power, e.g. particle accelerators, camera flashes 

Capacitors can also be used to turn AC into DC 
This is done using a diode to stop negative current and a capacitor to store charge that is then discharged during intervals of negative current 
This is useful for things like mobile phones and home appliances
