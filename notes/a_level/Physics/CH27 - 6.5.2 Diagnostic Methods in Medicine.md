#Physics #Medical 

X-rays or Gamma rays can be used to focus high energy on a concentrated part of the body 
This can be used to kill cancerous cells 

A medical tracer is a combination of elements and a radioactive isotope 
The combination of elements makes sure the tracer arrives at the correct place in the body 
The radioactive isotope will emit gamma radiation that can be detected from outside the body 
Factors to consider with tracers : 
- Needs to be low ionising radiation to minimise harm to patient (gamma radiation) 
- Needs to penetrate through the patient so it can be detected by instruments (gamma) 
- Needs to have a short half-life to minimise prolonged exposure for the patient 
- Needs to decay into a non-toxic and stable isotope 

Technetium-99m : 
- Emits gamma radiation 
- Has a half life of 6 hours 
- Decays into stable Technetium-99 
- Used for gamma cameras (needs to be made on-site due to half life) 

A gamma camera captures the concentration of gamma radiation to form an image : 
- The collimator absorbs photons at an angle to the camera so all incoming photons are in line 
- The scintillator converts gamma photons into thousands of photons of visible light 
- The photomultiplier tubes converts the light photons into electrical pulses / voltage 
- A computer system uses the voltage at different positions to create a high quality image

Gamma cameras are used to show the function and processes of the body 
X-ray cameras are used to show the anatomy of the body 

Fluorine-18 : 
- Emits a positron ($\beta^+$ decay) 
- Has a half life of 2 hours 
- Decays into stable Oxygen 
- Used for PET scans (needs to be made on-site with a particle accelerator) 

Positron Emission Tomography (PET) scanning creates a 3D image 
- Patient is injected with tracer and is surrounded by a ring of gamma detectors 
- Tracer decays and emits a positron, which annihilates producing 2 gamma photons 
- Computer can determine position of annihilation based on time difference and speed of the two emitted gamma photons (which travel in opposite directions) 
- Many 2D scans are layered to create the 3D image

Tracers for PET scans are often combined with glucose which goes to respiring tissues 

Advantages and disadvantages of PET scans : 
- Non-invasive technique (not exposed to risks of surgery) 
- Useful for diagnosing different types of cancer and size of tumours 
- Can be used to observe the brain and heart and to inform surgeries 
- Used to asses effects of new medicine / drugs on the body 
- Very expensive facilities to produce the medical tracers
