The photoelectric effect is when high-energy EM radiation emit electrons from metals 
Photoelectric effect provides evidence for the photon model

The photoelectric effect is a one-to-one interaction between a photon and an electron 
Einstein's photoelectric equation : $hf=\phi+KE_{max}$ 
The work function ($\phi$) of a metal is the minimum energy required to free a surface electron 
As the equation shows, energy from the photon beyond the work function is converted into KE 
Threshold frequency is the frequency $f_0$ such that $hf_0=\phi$ 
If frequency is below threshold frequency, no electrons are emitted 

Changing intensity of incident radiation means changing the number of inbound photons 
This will change how many electrons are emitted but will not change their maximum KE 
Above the threshold frequency, Intensity $\propto$ number of photoelectrons
