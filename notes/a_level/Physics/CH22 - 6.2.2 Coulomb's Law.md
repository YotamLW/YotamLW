#Physics #Electric 

Coulomb's Law is $F = \frac{Qq}{4 \pi \varepsilon_0 r^2}$ 
This is the force experienced between two charged particles 

Electric field strength $E = \frac{Q}{4 \pi \varepsilon_0 r^2}$ for a point charge 
Electric fields are essentially the same as gravitational fields except that masses always produce an attractive field whereas charges can create an attractive or repulsive field 
Both electric and gravitational fields can create radial fields
