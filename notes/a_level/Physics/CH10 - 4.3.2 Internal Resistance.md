#Physics #Electric 

Internal resistance is the resistance within a power supply 
Lost volts is the p.d. taken up by the internal resistor 
Terminal p.d. is e.m.f. minus lost volts 

$\mathcal{E} = IR + Ir$ or $\mathcal{E} = V + Ir$ where $r$ is internal resistance 

Finding internal resistance investigation : 
- Set up circuit with cell and variable resistor 
- Record different values for $V$ and $I$ 
- Plot a $V$ on $I$ graph 
- Re-arrange $\mathcal{E} = V + Ir$ to $V = - Ir + \mathcal{E}$ 
- Therefore y-intercept is the actual e.m.f. of the cell 
- And the gradient is equal to $-r$
