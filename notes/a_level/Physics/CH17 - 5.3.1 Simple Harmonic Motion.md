#Physics #Radial 

Definitions : 
- Displacement - distance from equilibrium position 
- Amplitude - maximum displacement from equilibrium position 
- Period - time taken for one oscillation 
- Frequency - number of oscillations per unit time 
- Phase difference ($\phi$) - difference in displacement between two oscillating objects 
Angular frequency $\omega = 2 \pi f$ or $\omega = \frac{2 \pi}{T}$ 

Simple harmonic motion occurs when $a = - \omega^2 x$ 
$\omega$ is a constant so $a \propto x$ 
Solving for $x$ : $x = A \cos \omega t$ or $x = A \sin \omega t$ depending on context 
Velocity $v = \pm \omega \sqrt{A^2 - x^2}$ and $v_{max} = \omega A$ 

The period of oscillations is independent of Amplitude (isochronous oscillator) 
Masses on springs or pendulums can be used to produce SHM
