#Physics #Radial 

Kepler's laws : 
1) The orbit of a planet is an ellipse with the star at one of the two foci 
2) A line segment joining a star and an orbiting planet will sweep out equal areas in equal intervals of time 
3) The square of the orbital period is directly proportional to the cube of the average orbital radius $T^2 \propto r^3$ 

Centripetal force of a planet is equal to the gravitational force of the star 
A geostationary satellite remains directly above the same point of the body it orbits 
Useful for communication satellites 

Derivation of $T^2 = (\frac{4 \pi^2}{G M}) r^3$ : 
- $\frac{m v^2}{r} = \frac{G M m}{r^2}$ 
- $v^2 = \frac{G M}{r}$ 
- Moving in a circle so $v = \frac{2 \pi r}{T}$ 
- $\frac{4 \pi^2 r^2}{T^2} = \frac{G M}{r}$ 
- $T^2 = (\frac{4 \pi^2}{G M}) r^3$
