#Physics #Waves 

The electromagnetic spectrum is the full range of EM waves : 
![[4.4.2a]] 
Wavelengths of different waves : 
- Radio waves - $\lambda>10^{-1}$ 
- Microwaves - $10^{-1}>\lambda>10^{-3}$ 
- Infrared - $10^{-3}>\lambda>7\times 10^{-7}$ 
- Visible - $7\times 10^{-7}>\lambda>4\times 10^{-7}$ 
- Ultraviolet - $4\times 10^{-7}>\lambda>10^{-8}$ 
- X-rays - $10^{-8}>\lambda>10^{-13}$ 
- Gamma rays - $10^{-10}>\lambda$ 

Properties of EM waves : 
- Transverse wave so can be plane polarised 
- All have the same speed of $3\times 10^8\;ms^{-1}\;(c)$ through a vacuum so $c=\lambda f$ 

Polarising filters allow only one plane of light to pass through 
Stages of rotating a filter over another filter : 
- When both upright all polarised light goes through 
- As top one is rotated, light level begins to decrease 
- At 90$^\circ$ no light goes through 
- As it is rotated further light level increases 
- At 180$^\circ$ all light goes through again 
![[4.4.2b]] 

Metal grilles polarise microwaves 
If direction of vibration is parallel to a grille, then the grille absorbs the wave 
![[4.4.2c]] 

Refractive index $n = \frac{c}{v}$ 
Refractive index in vacuum = refractive index in air = 1 
At a boundary, $n\sin\theta=\text{constant}$ where $\theta$ is the angle to the normal 
At a boundary between 2 mediums, $n_1\sin\theta_1=n_2\sin\theta_2$ 
![[4.4.2d]] 

Total internal reflection is when all light is reflected instead of refracted 
The critical angle $C=\arcsin\frac{1}{n}$ : 
- $\theta<C$ - refraction and partial reflection occur 
- $\theta=C$ - light refracts along the boundary between the two media and some partial reflection 
- $\theta>C$ - total internal reflection
