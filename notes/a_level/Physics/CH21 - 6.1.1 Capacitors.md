#Physics #Electric 

Capacitors are a circuit component consisting of two metal plates separated by a gap 
A capacitor has infinite resistance and electrons build up on one plate, creating a p.d. 
A capacitor acts as a cell that loses p.d. over time 

The capacitance of a capacitor $C = \frac{Q}{V}$ so $Q = VC$ 
Capacitance has the unit farad ($F$) 

Capacitors in parallel : 
- P.D. is the same for each capacitor 
- Total charge is the sum of all charges 
- $C_T = C_1 + C_2 + C_3 + ...$ 

Capacitors in series : 
- Charge is the same for each capacitor 
- P.D. is split between capacitors 
- $C_T = ( C_1^{-1} + C_2^{-1} + C_3^{-1} + ...)^{-1}$
