Specific heat capacity is the energy required per unit mass to increase the temperature of a substance by 1 K so has units $Jkg^{-1}K^{-1}$ 
$E = mc\Delta\theta$ where $c$ is specific heat capacity 

The method of mixtures is a way to determine specific heat capacity using an additional substance of known specific heat capacity at a different temperature 

Method of investigating specific heat capacity : 
- Set up circuit with a heater and find power output 
- Record temperature of material at different times 
- Plot a temperature on time graph 
- Draw straight line of best fit and find gradient 
- $E = mc\Delta\theta$ so $\frac{E}{\Delta t} = mc \frac{\Delta\theta}{\Delta t}$ so $P = mc \times \text{gradient}$ so $c = \frac{P}{m \times \text{gradient}}$ 

Specific latent heat of fusion is the energy required per unit mass to change a substance from a solid to a liquid 
Specific latent heat of vaporisation is the same but from a liquid to a gas 
$E = ml$ where $l$ is specific latent heat 

Method of investigating specific latent heat : 
- Set up circuit with a heater and find power output 
- Stick the heater in ice and collect melted water for a set time 
- Find mass of ice and energy output of heater then $l = \frac{E}{m}$ 
- This is specific latent heat of fusion 
- For specific latent heat of vaporisation, replace ice with water and use a closed flask with a condenser to capture the water for weighing
