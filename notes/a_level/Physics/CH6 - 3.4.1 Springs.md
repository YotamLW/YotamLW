#Physics #Materials 

Deformation is when objects stretch or squish 
Extension - Tensile forces 
Compression - Compressive forces 

Hooke's Law : Spring extension is directly proportional to force applied 
$F \propto x$ or $F = kx$ where k is the force constant 
Higher k means a stiffer material
