#Physics #Thermal 

Thermal equilibrium is when there is no net thermal energy transfer between objects 
An absolute scale of temperature is one where 0 is an object with no temperature (no energy) 
An absolute scale of temperature does not depend on the properties of any particular substance 

The thermodynamic scale uses Kelvin (K) and is an absolute scale of temperature 
Kelvin has the same scaling as Celsius so $\Delta 1 \; K = \Delta 1 \; ^{\circ}C$ and 0 K $\approx$ 273 $^{\circ}C$
