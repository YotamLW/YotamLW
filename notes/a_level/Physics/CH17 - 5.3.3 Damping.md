#Physics #Radial 

Free oscillation is when an object is oscillating with no external forces 
Frequency of free oscillation is the natural frequency of the object 
Forced oscillation is when a periodic driving force is applied to the object 
The object will oscillate at the driving frequency 

Damping is when an external force acts on the oscillator to reduce amplitude over time

Resonance occurs when driving frequency = natural frequency of the forced oscillator 
Examples of resonance : 
- Musical instruments e.g. pitch forks 
- Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) machines 
- Quartz crystals inside clocks 
- Breaking wine glasses
