#Physics #Energy 

The unit of Power is the Watt (W) 
Power is work done over time $P = \frac{W}{t}$ 
A Watt is equal to 1 Joule of energy transferred in 1 second 

$P = Fv$ 
Derivation : 
- $W = Fd$ and $P = \frac{W}{t}$ 
- $P = \frac{Fd}{t} = F\frac{d}{t} = Fv$ 

Efficiency of a system = $\frac{\text{useful output}}{\text{total input}} \times 100$%
