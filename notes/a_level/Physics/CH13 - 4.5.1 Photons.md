#Physics #Quantum 

The photon model is used to explain how electromagnetic waves interact with matter 
A photon is a quantum of energy of electromagnetic radiation 

The energy of a photon $E=hf$ or $E=\frac{hc}{\lambda}$ 
The electronvolt ($eV$) is a unit of energy equal to $1.6\times 10^{-19}\;J$ 

Investigation to determine the Planck constant : 
- Connect LED that emits light of a know wavelength $\lambda$ to a circuit 
- Turn up voltage slowly until LED just turns on 
- Assume LED is emitting a single photon, so $eV=hf$ or $eV=\frac{hc}{\lambda}$ 
- Repeat experiment with LEDs of different wavelengths 
- Rearrange equation to $V=\frac{1}{\lambda}\times\frac{hc}{e}$ 
- Plot a straight-line graph of $V$ on $\frac{1}{\lambda}$ so gradient = $\frac{hc}{e}$ 
- So $h=\text{gradient}\times\frac{e}{c}$
