#Physics #Motion 

The principle of conservation of momentum : 
For a closed system of interacting objects, momentum in any direction is conserved 

| Type of collision | Momentum  | Total Energy | Kinetic Energy |
| ----------------- | --------- | ------------ | -------------- |
| Perfectly Elastic | Conserved | Conserved    | Conserved      |
| Inelastic         | Conserved | Conserved    | Not Conserved  |
Use principle of conservation of momentum to evaluate collisions in 1 & 2 dimensions
