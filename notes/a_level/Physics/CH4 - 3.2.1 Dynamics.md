#Physics #Force 

Force = mass $\times$ acceleration : $F = ma$ 
The unit of force is the Newton (N) 
Force is written in S.I. base units as $kgms^{-1}$ 
An objects weight $W = mg$ 

Tension is when a force is pulling an object apart 
Normal contact force is the force pushing back against an object in contact 
Friction is the force that goes against motion 
Upthrust is the upward buoyant force when an object is submerged in a fluid 

A free-body diagram isolates an object and all forces acting on it
