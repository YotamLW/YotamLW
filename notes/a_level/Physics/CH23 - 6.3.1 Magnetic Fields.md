#Physics #Magnetism 

The Earth has a magnetic field which protects life from solar wind 
Magnetic fields move from north to south 
Magnetic fields are produced by permanent magnets or moving charges 
The magnetic field is stronger if field lines are closer together 
An X is a field going into the page and a dot is a field coming out of the page

Right-hand grip rule shows which way a magnetic field acts around a current through a wire 
- Thumb is direction of current, fingers is direction of magnetic field 
Fleming's left-hand rule shows the relationship between current, magnetic field and force 
- Thumb is force, second finger is direction of magnetic field, third finger is direction of current 

Force on a current carrying conductor is $F = BILsin(\theta)$ 
Where $\theta$ is the angle between the magnetic field and direction of current 
$B$ is magnetic flux density (unit Tesla $T$) 

$B$ can be found by rearranging to $B = \frac{F}{IL}$ and placing magnets on some scales. The force exerted can be found using the scales, the current can be measured using an ammeter and the length of wire in the magnetic field can be measured with a ruler
