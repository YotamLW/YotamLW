#Physics #Radial 

The force between two masses is $F = -\frac{GMm}{r^2}$ (Newton's law of gravitation) 
Where G = gravitational constant, r = separation and M & m are the two masses 
The gravitational field strength of a point mass is $g = -\frac{GM}{r^2}$ 
Close to the surface of the Earth, gravitational field strength is assumed to be uniform and $\approx$ 9.81
