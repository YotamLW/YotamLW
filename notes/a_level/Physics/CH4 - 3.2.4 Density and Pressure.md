#Physics #Force 

Density $\rho = \frac{m}{V}$ where m is mass and V is volume 
Pressure $p = \frac{F}{A}$ where F is force and A is cross-sectional area 

Archimedes' principle : 
The upthrust a fluid exerts on an object is equal to the weight of the fluid the object displaces 

Pressure of a fluid at a depth $h$ below its top is given as $p = h \rho g$
