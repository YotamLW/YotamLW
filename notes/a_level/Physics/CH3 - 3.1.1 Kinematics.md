#Physics #Motion 

Displacement is a vector and is the straight-line distance between two points 
Average speed = $\frac{\text{distance travelled}}{\text{time}}$ (scalar) 
Velocity = $\frac{\text{displacement}}{\text{time}}$ (vector) 
Acceleration = $\frac{\text{velocity}}{\text{time}}$ (vector) 

In displacement - time graphs velocity is the gradient 
In velocity - time graphs acceleration is the gradient and area under the graph is displacement 
Data-loggers can be used to analyse motion
