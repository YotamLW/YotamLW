#Physics #Nuclear 

Einstein's mass-energy equation : $\Delta E=\Delta mc^2$ 

When particle-antiparticle pairs meet (e.g. electron and positron) they annihilate 
This annihilation releases energy equal to the sum of their masses 
A particle-antiparticle pair can also be produced by pair production from a single photon 

Mass defect is the difference between the masses of a nucleus and its separated nucleons 
Binding energy is the minimum energy needed to separate the nucleons from a nucleus 
Binding energy per nucleon is binding energy divided by number of nucleons

Induced fission reactions are when thermal neutrons are absorbed by an isotope (like Uranium) to make it unstable and decay 
Uranium-235 absorbs a neutron and becomes Uranium-236, which decays into two daughter cells and 3 neutrons which are then absorbed by other Uranium-235 isotopes causing a chain reaction 

A fission reactor core contains the Uranium fuel rods and control rods 
Control rods absorb neutrons and can be moved in and out of the reactor core 
Control rods are needed because uncontrolled fission leads to an exponential chain reaction 
A coolant is used to extract thermal energy from the reaction 
Fuel rods are surrounded by a moderator which slows down emitted neutrons 
Neutrons need to be slowed down because slower neutrons are more likely to be absorbed 
The moderator is also the coolant in some reactors

Uranium-238 decays into Plutonium which is very toxic and radioactive 
Spent fuel rods are encased in concrete then buried deep underground to minimise impact on the surrounding environment and prevent leakage 

Nuclear fusion is when nuclei are combined into heavier nuclei to gain the mass deficit 
The nuclei need to be brought close enough for the strong nuclear force to pull them together 
The challenge is overcoming the repulsive electrostatic force between the nuclei 
This is overcome by high temperature and high pressure bringing the nuclei closer together 
The temperature at which fusion occurs in the core of the Sun is about $\pu{10^7 K}$
