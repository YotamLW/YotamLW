#Physics #Magnetism 

Force on a charged particle travelling at right angles to a uniform magnetic field is $F = BQv$ 
Use Fleming's left hand rule to determine direction of force 
Charged particles go in a circle when moving perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field 
Direction of current is direction of movement for positive charges and opposite for negative 

A velocity selector uses electric fields to accelerate a charged particles then uses a magnetic field to create circular motion in order to determine the particle's mass / charge
