#Physics #Electric 

Kirchhoff's second law (conservation of energy) : 
In any circuit, the sum of e.m.f. is equal to the sum of p.d. or $\Sigma \mathcal{E} = \Sigma V$ 

In series : 
- Current is constant throughout the circuit 
- Potential difference is divided across resistors such that $\frac{V_1}{V_2} = \frac{R_1}{R_2}$ 
- Total resistance of resistors $R = R_1 + R_2 + ...$ 

In parallel : 
- Current is divided between circuits (find p.d. and resistance first) 
- Potential difference is the same for each independent circuit 
- Total resistance of resistors $R  = \frac{1}{\frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2} + ...}$ 

If cells are placed in series, overall e.m.f. is added (or subtracted if facing opposite directions) 
If cells are placed in parallel, overall e.m.f. is the same because p.d. is the same for each circuit
