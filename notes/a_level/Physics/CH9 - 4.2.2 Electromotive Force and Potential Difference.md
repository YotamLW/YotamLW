#Physics #Electric 

Potential Difference - The energy transferred from electrical energy to other forms (or work done by charge carriers) per unit charge 
Electromotive Force - The energy transferred from chemical energy to electrical energy (or work done on charge carriers) per unit charge 

The unit of p.d. and e.m.f. is the volt 
One volt is the p.d. across a component when 1 J of energy is transferred by 1 C of charge 
The symbol of e.m.f. is $\mathcal{E}$ 

Energy transfer $W = VQ$ & $W = \mathcal{E}Q$ 

For a charged particle accelerated across a p.d. its energy would be $eV$ 
Therefore $eV = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$
