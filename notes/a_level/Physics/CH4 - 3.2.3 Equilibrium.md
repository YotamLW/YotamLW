#Physics #Force 

Moment = Force $\times$ displacement 
Force must be perpendicular to displacement 
A couple is a pair of forces equal in magnitude but opposite in direction so produce a moment (and thus rotation) but have no resultant force 
The moment of a couple is called torque 

Principle of moments : An object in equilibrium has a resultant force and resultant moment of 0 

An object's centre of mass is the average position of it's entire mass 
An object's centre of gravity is the point where resultant force appears to act 
A vertical line through the pivot of a suspended object will always pass the centre of gravity as well 
A plumb line can be used to draw a vertical line onto an object to determine its centre of gravity 

An object is in equilibrium if there is no moment or resultant force (principle of moments) 
A force triangle can be made to determine if resultant force is 0
