#Computer-Science 

Types of issues : 
- Moral - Concerns about personal sense of right & wrong 
- Ethical - Dilemmas based on accepted standards 
- Social - Challenge's affecting society's well-being 
- Cultural - Conflicts arising from cultural differences 

In relation to the use of computers : 
- Moral - Rights and wrongs about the use of computers 
- Ethical - Standards for responsible computing / use of computers 
- Social - Impact of technology on society 
- Cultural - Impact of technology on cultural values
