#Computer-Science 

## Data Protection Act 

Data must be : 
- Obtained and used lawfully 
- Relevant and not excessive 
- Accurate and up-to-date 
- Kept no longer than necessary 
- Kept secure 
- Only be used for purpose of collection 
- Transferred outside the EU with adequate protection 
- Available to individual provider to access / check / update 

## Computer Misuse Act 

A criminal offence to : 
- Gaining unauthorised access to a computer / device 
- Unauthorised acts intended to impair a computer / device 
- Unauthorised modification of computer material 
- Make, supply or obtain tools intended for 1, 2 or 3 
- Supply tools likely to be used for 1, 2 or 3 

Fines up to £5000 and jail time up to 10 years 
Tools include hardware & software 

## Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 

Protects intellectual property 
Illegal to use, modify or distribute without permission / license 
When buying a copyrighted / patented product you are buying the license 
Applies to literature, audio, images, software, inventions etc. 

Patents protect inventions 
Trademarks protect brand and logos 
Copyright is default on anything you create 

Makes the following actions crimes : 
- Unauthorised copying, distribution or performance of copyrighted works 
- Unauthorised use or reproduction of a registered design 
- Unauthorised use of patented inventions 

Unlimited fines and jail time up to 10 years  

## Regulation of Investigatory Powers Act 

Provides specific public bodies with regulated rights to : 
- Demand that ISPs provide secret access to communications and fit surveillance equipment 
- Mass surveil communications in transit 
- Demand individuals provide keys to unlock protected information 
- Monitor individuals' internet activities 
- Prevent details of interception from being revealed in court 

Gives public bodies (e.g. police) power to carry out investigations 
Regulates those powers to prevent corruption through guidelines and authorisation
