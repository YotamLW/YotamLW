#Mathematics #Core 

A sequence is a list of numbers generated using a formula 
A series is the sum of a sequence 

Types of sequences : 
- Increasing - terms go up 
- Decreasing - terms go down 
- Periodic - terms repeat every certain increment of n 
- Convergent - terms converge closer and closer to a single value 
- Divergent - terms do not converge to a single value (includes periodic with order > 1) 

## Arithmetic 

Arithmetic sequence : $a_n = a + (n - 1) d$ where $a$ is the first term and $d$ is the common difference 
The sum of an arithmetic sequence $S_n = \frac{1}{2} n (2a + (n - 1) d)$ 
An alternative formula is $S_n = \frac{1}{2} n (a + l)$ where $l$ is the final term of the sequence 

## Geometric 

Geometric sequence : $a_n = a \times r^{n-1}$ where $r$ is the common difference 
The sum of a geometric sequence $S_n = \frac{a (1 - r^n)}{1 - r}$ 
For a geometric series to be convergent $|r| < 1$ 
Sum of convergent series to infinity $S_\infty = \frac{a}{1-r}$
