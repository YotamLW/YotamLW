A-Level Notes

I made all my notes on obsidian with excalidraw for images. For optimal viewing you should install the obsidian desktop app then install the excalidraw community plugin, or just view the markdown files and images separately. The notes can be found on github at /notes/a_level

This directory contains notes I made for my A level exams in Maths, Further Maths, Physics and Computer science. All my exams were by the OCR exam board and taken in 2025 (more details below). These notes are not self-containing; I made them to help me remember mainly facts rather than how to go about a question. The way I made these notes was reading through the official long-form specification for each subject then using available resources to write about it until I felt confident in each spec-point. If you use these notes I would recommend reading the long-form spec. In the future I plan to use these ntoes and other sources to make a self-containing "curriculum" on my webie project.

Mathematics (A*)

I did OCR Mathematics B (Mei) H640. The notes include a partially complete core section, no mechanics section and a fully complete statistics section. The letter + number prefixes are based on topic naming at my school.

Further Mathematics (A*)

I did OCR Further Mathematics B (Mei) H645, with core and 3 minors - mechanics, statistics and extra pure. The core section is partially complete. Mechanics, statistics and extra pure are all fully complete. Prefixes same as maths.

Physics (A*)

I did OCR Physics A H556A. I made notes on everything, and images :D. The prefixes are the chapter in the book "A Level Physics for Cambridge OCR A" followed by the specification point.

Computer Science (A)

I did OCR Computer Science H446A. I made notes on everything except the wordy topics. The prefixes are the chapter in the book "OCR AS and A Level Computer Science" (don't recommend the book, recommend Craig n' Dave on youtube) followed by the specification point. Despite the book chapter naming, I preferred other sources (my teacher's slides) over the book.
