Thoughts on Subjects

For my A-levels I did Maths, Further Maths, Physics and Computer Science and I am now studying Systems Engineering at University. During A-levels, I formed the idea that maths is basically just abstract logic, physics is physically applied maths and computer science is abstractly applied maths. Of course this is a very simplified view but it kind of connects these topics together nicely and helps me choose how to structure these notes. I see engineering as the subject that applies all these subjects to the real world (with the individual subjects more so about finding new ways to apply theories rather than applying them). Anyhow, this is the reason I will often cross reference different subjects, because I think they are all related and there is just a lot of overlap. Once I have more topics I plan to add a "tech tree" kind of graph for seeing topic dependencies and uses.
