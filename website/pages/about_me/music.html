Supertramp

Supertramp is currently my favourite band, so I wanted to share my favourite songs. This is probably likely to change because I seem to be liking the songs more as I listen to them more. For context, I'm pretty sure I like songs with significant contrast, or that have a mix of bass and tenor rather than an oversaturation of either. I suspect it's because it's only him singing and the instrumentation (and vocals) become more stable which I think removes the contrast I liked about Supertramp. Sometimes I think it might be the drums being too consistent in more modern songs that make me like them less; something about the repeated bass rythms that goes in cycles and doesn't stop throughout the whole song. I also listened to the 4 later Supertramp albums with only the 4 members and I found most of the songs also had the same problem for me with lack of contrast, though in blues rather than high energy (especially the last 2 albums). I think the 'Free As A Bird' album was quite good though, the best of the 4, and liked the songs 'It's Alight', 'Free As A Bird' and 'An Awful Thing To Waste' from it.
